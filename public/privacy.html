隐私政策

了解你的数据如何帮助你学习，以及如何停止授权或申请删除。

更新日期：2026 年 10 月 28 日 · English version ↓

1. 适用范围与联系

本政策适用于 studybridge.ink 上的 StudyBridge 学习服务。数据用途、访问、更正、导出或删除请求，请联系 StudyBridge 项目运营方：qinmingyu4@gmail.com。处理请求前，我们会核对账号归属，请勿通过邮件发送密码或授权令牌。

2. 我们处理哪些数据

账号数据包括邮箱、显示名称、头像、个人资料、偏好和登录会话。使用学习功能时，我们处理你提交的课程资料、对话、文件、社区或私聊内容，以及日程。社区发布和私聊内容会提供给你选择的交流对象；邮箱数据不会自动发布到社区或分享给同学。

Google 登录与 Gmail 绑定分开授权。Google 登录使用基本身份信息，不会自动授予读取邮件的权限。我们不要求或保存 Google / Microsoft 邮箱密码。

3. Gmail 与 Outlook 邮箱助手

绑定邮箱是可选功能，必须在“设置 → 邮箱授权”明确同意，并在 Google 或 Microsoft 页面授权。Gmail 使用 gmail.readonly；Outlook 使用本人邮箱的 Mail.Read、基本账号信息和后台续期权限。我们不申请发送、修改或删除邮箱邮件的权限。

连接后，服务器会定期检查收件箱，首次检查最近 7 天。处理内容包括邮箱地址、邮件标识、主题、发件人、收件时间和正文文本；暂不读取附件。为判断是否与学习相关，收件箱邮件文本会交给 AI 处理，包括最终被判定为不相关的邮件。提取的学习通知保存主题、发件人、摘要、原文片段、来源链接和建议事项，供你核对。系统不会自动把提取结果加入日程，必须由你确认。

4. 处理服务与用途限制

StudyBridge 使用 AWS 托管服务和数据存储；使用 OpenAI API 处理你请求的 AI 功能。邮箱识别会向 OpenAI API 发送邮件主题、发件人、时间及正文文本，申请不保存该次 AI 生成结果（store: false）；这不等于第三方服务完全不保留安全或运行记录。数据可能在你所在国家或地区以外处理。Google 和 Microsoft 分别提供身份认证与邮箱 API。

Google 用户数据及其衍生内容仅用于你请求的、面向用户的功能。StudyBridge 不出售这些数据，不用于广告、信用评估，也不用于训练、微调或改进通用 AI / 机器学习模型。向服务提供商传输数据仅为提供必要功能，并受适用数据用途限制约束。人工访问仅限你对特定内容明确同意、必要的安全调查、适用法律要求，或符合 Google 政策的内部汇总且匿名化用途。

StudyBridge's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. 保存与安全

授权令牌在服务器端以 AES-256-GCM 加密保存，通过 HTTPS 与邮箱及 AI 服务通信。加密令牌不表示全部学习资料和提取记录均使用同样的字段加密。我们保留提供账号和学习功能所需的数据；邮箱提取记录保留到解除绑定，已确认日程、其他学习资料和账号记录保留到你删除相关内容或提出删除请求。

私有运维备份可能保留较早副本，解除绑定不会立即清除所有备份。删除请求可以包含私有备份；运营方需要处理相应副本，并遵守必要的安全及法律留存要求。第三方服务的留存还受其服务条款和数据政策约束。

6. 撤销、删除与控制

在“设置 → 邮箱授权”解除绑定，会停止后续同步，移除在线连接凭据及相应提取记录；已经确认的日程保留，由你单独管理。你也可以在 Google 账号的第三方连接或 Microsoft 账号中撤销授权。仅在提供方撤销权限不会自动删除 StudyBridge 已保存的数据，仍应在 StudyBridge 解除绑定或申请删除。

如需删除账号、导出数据或处理备份中的副本，请联系上述邮箱。不会因为拒绝邮箱授权而禁止你使用其他不依赖邮箱的学习功能。

7. Cookie 与更新

我们使用必要的会话 Cookie 维持登录及保护授权流程，使用浏览器存储保存界面偏好和部分编辑草稿。本政策更新会在本页标注日期；扩大邮箱用途或权限时需重新告知并取得相应授权。

ENGLISH VERSION

StudyBridge Privacy Policy

Updated October 28, 2026. Contact the StudyBridge project operator at qinmingyu4@gmail.com for access, correction, export or deletion requests. We verify account ownership; never email passwords or OAuth tokens.

Data and optional mailbox access

We process account identifiers, profile and preferences, sessions, and the course materials, conversations, files, community posts, private messages and schedules you provide. Posts and messages are shared with their intended audience. Mailbox data is not automatically shared with classmates or the community. Google sign-in uses basic identity information and does not authorize Gmail access.

Mailbox access requires separate consent in Settings and at Google or Microsoft. Gmail uses gmail.readonly. Outlook uses delegated Mail.Read, basic profile information and offline access. We do not request permission to send, modify or delete email. We check Inbox messages periodically, initially looking back seven days. We process the mailbox address, message identifiers, subject, sender, receipt time and body text, but not attachments. To classify relevance, message text is sent to AI, including messages subsequently classified as unrelated to learning. Relevant notices retain source details, summaries, short evidence excerpts, links and proposed items. Adding an item to your StudyBridge schedule requires your confirmation.

Processors and Limited Use

AWS hosts the service and stored data. OpenAI API processes requested AI features; email classification transmits the subject, sender, time and body text with store: false. This setting does not mean the provider retains no security or operational records. Google and Microsoft provide authentication and mailbox APIs. Processing may take place outside your country.

Google user data and derived content are used only to provide the user-facing features you request. StudyBridge does not sell this data or use it for advertising, credit assessment, or training, fine-tuning or improving generalized AI / machine learning models. Transfers to service providers are limited to necessary feature delivery and applicable use restrictions. Human access is limited to your affirmative agreement to view specific content, necessary security investigations, applicable law, or internal aggregated and anonymized uses permitted by Google policy.

StudyBridge's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention, security and your controls

OAuth tokens are encrypted server-side using AES-256-GCM; HTTPS protects communication with mailbox and AI providers. This does not claim identical field-level encryption for every stored learning record. Extraction records are retained until disconnection. Confirmed schedule entries, other learning content and account records remain until you delete the relevant content or request deletion. Private operational backups may retain earlier copies; disconnection does not immediately erase every backup. You may request deletion covering private backups, subject to necessary security and legal retention. Third-party retention is also governed by those providers' terms and policies.

Disconnect in Settings → Mailbox authorization to stop subsequent sync and remove active connection credentials and extracted records. Confirmed schedule items remain under your control. You may also revoke access through Google third-party connections or your Microsoft account. Provider-side revocation alone does not erase previously stored StudyBridge data: disconnect here or request deletion as well. Contact us for account deletion, export, or backup-copy deletion. Declining mailbox access does not prevent use of unrelated features.

Essential session cookies support login and OAuth security; browser storage retains interface preferences and some drafts. We date changes on this page and seek appropriate new consent before expanding mailbox purposes or permissions.
